Let’s tackle the Pricing Table Patterns. Pricing is usually the most rigid part of a site, so making it "funky" while keeping it functional is a great design challenge for your dev tools.
In your universe, these aren't just static tables; they are interactive value-calculators that reflect the personality of the post type (e.g., a "Pro" plan for a Podcast vs. an "Elite" tier for an Event). [1, 2, 3] 
Pricing Table Patterns: 5 Funky Styles1. The "Bento-Switch" (Modular)

* Visual: A grid of 3 tiles. The "Recommended" plan is 2x the size of the others and sits in a glowing Glassmorphism container.
* Interaction: A giant, tactile toggle (Form Theme: Industrial Breaker) at the top switches all prices between "Monthly" and "Yearly" with a mechanical "click" animation.
* Best For: SaaS Pages or Service Portfolios. [4, 5, 6] 

2. The "Receipt Scroll" (Brutalist)

* Visual: Long, narrow vertical strips that look like thermal printer receipts with "jagged" edges at the bottom (Divider Style 01).
* Interaction: On hover, the "receipt" extends downward to reveal a detailed feature list in Monospace font.
* Best For: Technical Posts or Events.

3. The "Liquid Slider" (Experimental)

* Visual: No tables. Just one large, centered card.
* Interaction: A horizontal slider at the bottom. As you slide it, the card "morphs" (Image Block Style 01) into different colors and shapes, updating the price and features dynamically.
* Best For: Creative Portfolio packages. [7, 8, 9, 10, 11] 

4. The "Arcade Cabinet" (Retro-Digital)

* Visual: Three neon-bordered boxes that look like arcade game screens.
* Interaction: Hovering over a plan triggers a "Level Up" sound effect and the button (Button Style 14: Arcade Switch) flashes "INSERT COIN."
* Best For: Video subscriptions or Gaming content. [12, 13] 

5. The "Floating Aura" (Minimalist)

* Visual: Ghostly, borderless text floating over a deep dark background.
* Interaction: When a plan is selected, a massive Neon Glow (Image Style 08) appears behind it, casting light onto the surrounding sections.
* Best For: High-end Consulting or Podcasts. [14] 

------------------------------
Pattern Dev Tool: "The Pricing Lab"

* Currency Switcher: A dropdown to test how $ vs. € vs. £ (and long numbers) fit in the funky boxes.
* Feature Stress-Test: A toggle to add 20+ features to see how the "Receipt" or "Bento" styles handle long-scrolling content.
* Comparison Mode: A "Highlight Differences" button that dims all features common to all plans, leaving only the unique "upsell" features glowing. [15]

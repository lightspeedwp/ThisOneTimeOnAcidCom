## Overview

This document defines the voice, tone, content strategy, and platform-specific guidelines for Ash Shaw's social media presence across Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn.

**Core principle:** Authenticity over polish. Community over commerce. Connection over conversion.

## Brand identity

### The multi-tribal identity

Ash's social media presence reflects the overlap of multiple communities:

- **Psytrance/140 BPM tribe** — dancefloor culture, festival vibes, UV art
- **WordPress community** — open source, web development, design systems
- **Berlin club scene** — techno, creative freedom, neurodivergent artist community
- **Endurance sports** — cycling, trail running, Muay Thai, triathlon
- **Six Cats** — organic cannabis cultivation, Cape Town garden culture
- **LightSpeed** — WordPress agency, remote work, AI-assisted workflows

**The overlap is the identity.** Content should weave between these worlds naturally, showing how they inform and enrich each other.

### Visual identity: Neon vs Atomic Black

- **Primary palette:** Neon pink, electric blue, toxic green, blazing orange, violet purple
- **Background:** Atomic black (#0F0F0F) or natural outdoor settings
- **Aesthetic:** Bold, vibrant, unapologetic. UV-reactive makeup, geometric designs, high-contrast neon
- **Photography:** Raw festival moments, cycling journeys, training sessions, UV paint in action

### Pronouns and identity

- **Pronouns:** He/Him (strictly enforced)
- **Locations:** Cape Town (home base) / Berlin (summers, late May–September) / Koh Phangan (training seasons)
- **Age:** Born 1981 (45 years old as of 2026)
- **Star sign:** Aquarius
- **Neurotype:** ADHD (frame as engine, not handicap)

## Voice and tone

### Voice attributes

1. **Authentic** — Raw, honest, unfiltered. No corporate speak. No pretense.
2. **Energetic** — Moves fast, like the ADHD brain. Short punchy sentences. Exclamation marks allowed.
3. **Educational** — Share knowledge freely. Open source mindset. Teach, don't gatekeep.
4. **Community-focused** — It's about the tribe, not the individual. Name people. Credit collaborators.
5. **Unapologetically neon** — Bold opinions. Strong visual language. "Neon vs Atomic Black" is a philosophy, not just a colour scheme.

### Tone variations by platform

**Instagram:**  
- Visual-first, story-driven  
- Behind-the-scenes energy  
- Festival recaps, UV paint process videos, cycling journey posts  
- Captions: personal, reflective, 2–4 sentences with a call to connection

**Facebook:**  
- Longer-form storytelling  
- Event recaps with context and gratitude  
- Cross-posting blog articles with commentary  
- Captions: 1–2 paragraphs, more narrative depth

**LinkedIn:**  
- Professional insights without losing personality  
- WordPress/design system content, open source contributions  
- LightSpeed updates, AI workflow experiments, team wins  
- Captions: educational, value-driven, but still authentic (not corporate)

### What to avoid

- ❌ Corporate/salesy language ("Elevate your brand", "Unlock your potential")
- ❌ Passive voice ("Faces were painted" → "I painted 40 faces")
- ❌ Overused hashtags (#blessed, #instagood, #livingmybestlife)
- ❌ Engagement bait ("Double tap if you agree!")
- ❌ Inauthentic positivity (toxic positivity is not the vibe)

- **Week 3:** WordPress/dev insights (Pillar 3)

## Engagement guidelines

### Responding to comments

- **Response rate goal:** 80% within 24 hours
- **Tone:** Warm, appreciative, authentic
- **Name people:** "Thanks Sarah!" not "Thanks!"
- **Ask questions back:** Turn comments into conversations

### DMs and collaboration requests

- **Makeup requests:** "I don't charge for my art — it's the pleasure of creating and seeing people shine. If we cross paths at a festival, come find me!"
- **WordPress/dev questions:** Generous with knowledge. Link to blog posts, GitHub repos, or offer a quick tip.
- **Commercial requests:** Politely decline. This is a personal art project, not a service business.

### Community building

- **Tag collaborators:** Festival organisers, other artists, WordPress contributors
- **Share others' work:** Repost festival recaps, highlight community members
- **Credit photographers:** Always tag/credit when using others' photos

## Platform-specific best practices

### Instagram

- **Image specs:** 1080×1080 (square), 1080×1350 (portrait), 1920×1080 (landscape)
- **Reels:** 9:16 vertical, 15–60 seconds, trending audio
- **Stories:** Raw, unpolished, behind-the-scenes energy
- **Highlights:** Organise into "UV Art", "Cycling", "WordPress", "Six Cats", "Tribes"

### Facebook

- **Image specs:** 1200×630 (link previews), 1080×1080 (square posts)
- **Events:** Create events for WordCamps, festivals (public appearances)
- **Albums:** Organise festival recaps, cycling journeys, project galleries
- **Cross-posting:** Share blog posts with 1–2 paragraph commentary

### LinkedIn

- **Image specs:** 1200×627 (article previews), 1080×1080 (square posts)
- **Articles:** Repurpose blog posts (WordPress, design systems, AI workflows)
- **Tone shift:** Still authentic, but emphasise professional insights
- **Tags:** Tag LightSpeed team members, WordPress community leaders

## Content approval workflow

1. **Draft content** in notes app or content calendar
2. **Self-review checklist:**
   - [ ] Sentence case for all headings/captions?
   - [ ] Pronouns correct (he/him)?
   - [ ] Voice authentic (no corporate speak)?
   - [ ] Credits/tags added?
   - [ ] Hashtags relevant and under 15?
3. **Schedule or post immediately** (depending on timing)
4. **Engage within first hour** (reply to early comments)

## Metrics that matter

### Vanity metrics (ignore)

- Follower count
- Total likes
- Impressions

### Meaningful metrics (track monthly)

- **Engagement rate** — Comments + saves + shares / followers
- **DM conversations** — Quality over quantity
- **Community growth** — Are the right people finding us?
- **Cross-platform referrals** — Instagram → blog, LinkedIn → GitHub, etc.

**Philosophy:** We're not trying to go viral. We're building a tribe.

---

## Crisis management

### If a post receives negative feedback

1. **Don't delete** (unless it violates platform rules or is factually incorrect)
2. **Respond calmly** — "Thanks for the feedback. Here's my perspective…"
3. **Know when to disengage** — Don't feed trolls

### If personal safety is compromised

1. **Block and report** abusive accounts immediately
2. **Document** (screenshot) before blocking
3. **Notify trusted contacts** if situation escalates

### If content is misinterpreted

1. **Clarify in comments** or a follow-up Story
2. **Own mistakes** — "You're right, I misspoke. Here's what I meant…"
3. **Learn and move forward**

## Legal and ethical considerations

### Cannabis content (Six Cats)

- ✅ **Legal context:** South Africa personal cultivation laws (Post-Constitutional Court 2018)
- ✅ **Educational framing:** Growing methods, organic practices, plant care
- ❌ **Avoid:** Sales language, pricing, cross-border shipping references
- ❌ **Platform rules:** Instagram can remove cannabis content — use discreet language if needed

### Music and festival content

- ✅ **Credit DJs and artists** in festival recap posts
- ✅ **Tag festival organisers** (Origin, Vortex, Sisyphos, etc.)
- ❌ **Copyright music in Reels** — use Instagram's licensed music library

### Personal boundaries

- ✅ **Share:** Creative process, training routines, travel adventures, community moments
- ❌ **Avoid:** Home address, real-time location during solo activities, personal conflicts

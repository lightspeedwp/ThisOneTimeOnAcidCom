## Event-driven content (add to calendar as needed)

### Festivals & club events

- **Post-event:** Recap post with photos, gratitude for organisers, tag people

- **Pre-event:** Share talk topic, invite community to attend
- **Live:** Behind-the-scenes Stories, speaker prep, networking moments
- **Post-event:** Key takeaways post, photos with community, link to slides/recording

- **Product launches:** Announce new plugin, design system release, open source contribution

## Content creation batching (efficiency strategy)

### Photography batching

- **Festival weekends:** Capture 20–30 shots for the month ahead
- **Training sessions:** Film 3–5 short clips per workout (bank for Reels)
- **Garden updates:** Monthly photo series of plant progress + cats

### Writing batching

- **Caption bank:** Draft 10–15 captions in one sitting (30–60 min)
- **Hashtag sets:** Pre-build hashtag groups for each pillar (save in notes)
- **Story scripts:** Outline 5–7 Story topics for the week (bullet points only)

### Scheduling strategy

- **Use tools:** Meta Business Suite (Instagram/Facebook), Buffer/Later (cross-platform)
- **Buffer time:** Schedule 2–3 days ahead (allows flexibility for real-time content)
- **Review queue:** Check scheduled posts every Sunday evening

## Cross-platform repurposing

### Blog post → Social media cascade

1. **LinkedIn:** Share full article link with 1–2 paragraph commentary (Week 1)
2. **Facebook:** Excerpt key section + link to full post (Week 2)
3. **Instagram:** Pull quote as graphic + carousel with 3–5 key points (Week 3)
4. **Instagram Stories:** Behind-the-scenes of writing the post (Week 1)

### Festival recap → Multi-format content

1. **Instagram Post:** Best UV transformation photo + short caption
2. **Instagram Reel:** 30-second montage of painting process
3. **Instagram Stories:** 5–7 frame series (arrival, setup, faces, dancefloor, sunrise, departure)
4. **Facebook:** Longer narrative post with photo album
5. **Blog:** Full festival diary with embedded photos (link back from social)

### Cycling journey → Documentary series

1. **Instagram Story (Day 1):** "Starting the ride" — bike loaded, route map
2. **Instagram Story (Day 2):** "Midpoint check-in" — stats, scenery, reflections
3. **Instagram Story (Day 3):** "Arrival" — festival entrance, unloading gear
4. **Instagram Post (Week after):** Journey recap with stats + hero image
5. **Blog (Month after):** Long-form journey essay with map, photos, lessons

- [ ] **Content gaps:** What topics did we miss?

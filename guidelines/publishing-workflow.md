## Overview

Internal reference for manuscript status, publication model, content maturity levels, and editorial workflow.

---

## Publication Stages

1. **Draft** - Work in progress, internal review
2. **Review** - Peer review phase
3. **Approved** - Ready for publication
4. **Published** - Live on portfolio
5. **Archived** - Historical reference

---

## Content Maturity Levels

- **Immature:** New, incomplete content
- **Developing:** Core content present, refinement needed
- **Stable:** Complete, tested content
- **Mature:** Widely used, minimal changes

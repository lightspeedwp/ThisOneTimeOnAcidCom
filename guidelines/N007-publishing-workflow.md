# N007: Publishing Workflow & Boundaries

**Purpose:** Manuscript status tracking, publication model, content maturity levels, and editorial workflow per D2 decision.

**Authority:** D4 (N-Document Approvals); D2 (Manuscript & Access Control)

---

## Content Maturity Levels

### Draft
- Internal working version
- May contain incomplete sections
- Not published to site
- Visibility: Editors only

### Ready for Review
- Complete but awaiting editorial review
- Structure and content finalized
- Visibility: Editors + designated reviewers

### Published
- Reviewed and approved for publication
- Live on site
- Visibility: Public

### Archived
- Superseded or deprecated content
- Removed from main navigation
- Visibility: Historical record (if needed)

---

## Editorial Workflow

1. **Write:** Author creates draft
2. **Peer Review:** Second author reviews
3. **Editorial Review:** Editor checks for brand voice and standards
4. **Publish:** Content goes live
5. **Monitor:** Track engagement and feedback
6. **Archive:** Remove or supersede when needed

---

## Publication Boundaries

- **Live Content:** Published articles visible to all users
- **Preview Access:** Unpublished content accessible via preview URL (editor-only)
- **Search Indexing:** Only published content indexed by search engines
- **Caching:** Published content cached globally; drafts not cached

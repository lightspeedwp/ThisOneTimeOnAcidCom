## Brand Palette

**Neon Colors:**
- Neon Pink: #FF006E
- Neon Cyan: #00F5FF
- Neon Yellow: #FFFF00
- Neon Green: #39FF14
- Neon Purple: #BC13FE

---

## Contrast Requirements

- **AAA Standard:** 7:1 minimum
- **WCAG Large Text:** 4.5:1 minimum
- **Dark Mode:** Ensure visibility both modes

---

## Color Usage

- **Primary:** Neon Pink (actions, highlights)
- **Secondary:** Neon Cyan (accents)
- **Success:** Neon Green
- **Warning:** Neon Yellow
- **Error:** Neon Pink

## Brand Identity

This guide defines the voice, tone, and messaging framework for all site content.

---

## Voice Principles

1. **Authentic** - Genuine, not corporate
2. **Accessible** - Clear language, jargon-free
3. **Confident** - Expertise without arrogance
4. **Empowering** - Elevates users and clients
5. **Creative** - Innovative approach to storytelling

---

## Tone Variations

- **Educational:** Teaching makeup techniques
- **Conversational:** Blog posts and journal entries
- **Professional:** Service descriptions
- **Inspirational:** Portfolio and case studies
- **Supportive:** FAQ and help sections

---

## Messaging Framework

### Core Message
Makeup artistry meets technical innovation for transformative portfolio experiences.

### Key Messaging
- Showcasing artistic excellence
- Accessible to all skill levels
- Portfolio-driven career growth
- Festival and event coverage

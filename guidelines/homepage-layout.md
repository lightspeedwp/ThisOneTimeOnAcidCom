## Homepage Structure

1. **Hero Section** - Featured image, headline, CTA
2. **Proposition Section** - Brand story, value props
3. **Featured Portfolio** - Showcase projects
4. **Latest Blog** - Recent journal entries
5. **Call-to-Action** - Final engagement
6. **Footer** - Navigation, contact

---

## Component Layout

- Hero: Full width, centered
- Content: 1200px max-width
- Grid: Responsive 2–4 columns
- Sections: Even spacing (md–lg)

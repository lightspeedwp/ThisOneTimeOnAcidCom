## The Green Garden

Welcome to the Six Cats garden. This is where nearly twenty years of cultivation practice meets organic living soil, rainwater harvesting, and six resident felines who run the operation.

### Meet the Cats

**Timmy** — The resident therapy cat. He appears on your lap during stressful moments and supervises every harvest.

**Wendy** — The agile matriarch. She navigates the garden like she owns it. Because she does.

**Jimmy** — The FIV fighter. Resilient, determined, and always near the compost bins.

**Bean** — The survivor. She arrived in the worst condition and transformed into the most confident cat in the house.

**Jeff** — The wanderer. He disappears for days, returning with the air of someone who's been on a tremendous journey.

**Frank** — Blue eyes. The observer. He watches from high perches and judges your composting technique.

### The Living Soil System

Watch the complete cultivation process:

**Bokashi composting:** Kitchen scraps fermented in sealed buckets with bokashi bran. After two weeks, the fermented material goes into the soil to feed the microbes.

**Worm farming:** Red wrigglers process organic matter into black gold. The worm castings are the foundation of the living soil.

**Worm tea:** Every two weeks during the growing season, we brew aerobic compost tea. Worm castings + water + oxygen + 48 hours = liquid microbial life. Applied as foliar spray and soil drench.

**Companion planting:** Basil grows between the plants. Natural pest control and beneficial insect attraction.

### The Cultivation Philosophy

Entirely organic. Nothing rushed. Nothing synthetic. Small-batch, hand-tended, high-quality flower grown outdoors using the natural cycles of the sun and seasons.

Rainwater harvested and triple-filtered. Cape Town almost ran out of water in 2018 — we take water seriously. 13,500 litres of storage capacity.

### The Eight Values

Every harvest season, these eight values guide the operation:

1. **Authenticity** — We grow what we say we grow
2. **Consciousness** — Every decision considers impact
3. **Consistency** — Same soil, same methods, same care
4. **Experience** — Nearly twenty years of growing
5. **Meticulousness** — Every plant observed daily
6. **Sustainability** — Rainwater, composting, glass reuse
7. **Passion** — This is a practice, not a side hustle
8. **Quality** — The grading system exists because quality is measurable

> Cannabis cultivation is an art form that rewards exactly the qualities I have in abundance: meticulousness, patience, obsessive attention to detail, and genuine respect for the natural process.

These are the same hands that paint neon faces at festivals. Both practices require patience, attention to detail, sensitivity to the subject, and deep respect for the process.

Six Cats is not a side project. It's a mirror.

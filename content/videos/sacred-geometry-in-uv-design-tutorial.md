## The Foundation of My Design Language

Sacred geometry isn't just pretty patterns. It's the mathematical language of nature, the blueprint of the universe, and the foundation of every UV design I create.

This tutorial breaks down three core geometric patterns — Flower of Life, Metatron's Cube, and Sri Yantra — and shows you how to adapt them to the contours of the human face.

### Why Sacred Geometry?

The answer is simple: **symmetry and meaning.**

Sacred geometry provides instant visual harmony. The patterns are inherently balanced, mathematically perfect, and deeply resonant. When painted on a face under UV light, they transform the person into a living mandala.

But it's more than aesthetics. These patterns carry cultural and spiritual significance — from ancient Egypt to Hindu yantra traditions to modern psytrance visual culture. Painting them is an act of connection.

### Pattern 1: Flower of Life

The foundational pattern. Seven overlapping circles creating a perfect geometric lattice.

**Construction steps:**
1. Start with a central circle (third eye or bridge of nose)
2. Draw six circles around the central point, each touching the centre
3. Extend outward in layers, maintaining perfect overlap
4. The pattern naturally creates petal shapes

**Facial adaptation:** Use the cheekbones as natural anchor points. Let the pattern flow across the face rather than fighting the contours.

### Pattern 2: Metatron's Cube

Thirteen circles connected by straight lines, forming a complex three-dimensional lattice within a two-dimensional surface.

**Construction steps:**
1. Start with the Flower of Life base
2. Connect the centres of each circle with straight lines
3. The result: nested geometric shapes (tetrahedrons, cubes, octahedrons)

**Pro tip:** Metatron's Cube works best on larger canvas areas — full face or extending onto the neck and shoulders.

### Pattern 3: Sri Yantra

The most complex pattern. Nine interlocking triangles radiating from a central point, representing the union of masculine and feminine energy.

**Construction steps:**
1. Start with a central bindu (dot) at the third eye
2. Draw four upward-pointing triangles (masculine)
3. Draw five downward-pointing triangles (feminine)
4. Surround with concentric circles and lotus petals

**Challenge:** Maintaining symmetry with nine triangles requires extreme precision. Practice on paper first.

### Maintaining Symmetry

The secret to perfect symmetry: **ambidextrous mirror-image painting.**

Both hands work simultaneously from the centre outward. Left hand paints the right side of the face. Right hand paints the left side. The movements mirror each other, creating organic symmetry.

### UV Colour Choices

Not all colours are equal under blacklight. Here's my go-to palette for sacred geometry:

- **Electric green:** Sharpest lines, maximum glow
- **Hot pink:** Soft, ethereal, dreamlike
- **Royal blue:** Deep, mysterious, grounding
- **Pure yellow:** Highlights and accent points only

> Sacred geometry transformed my practice from face painting to art form. The patterns provide structure, meaning, and instant visual impact.

This is the foundation. Master these three patterns, and you can create infinite variations. The geometry is the grammar. Your creativity is the language.

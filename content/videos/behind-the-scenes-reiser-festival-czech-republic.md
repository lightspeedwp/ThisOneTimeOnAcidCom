## The Reality Behind the Art

Everyone sees the finished UV transformations glowing under blacklight. This video shows what happens behind the scenes at a multi-day festival in the Czech mountains.

### Setup

Watch the full setup process: finding the right spot, securing the workspace, organizing the kit. Everything needs to be accessible in the dark. Brushes, paints, mirror stand, lights, water, towels — all within arm's reach.

The workspace becomes my home for the next three days.

### The 12-Hour Sessions

Festival painting isn't a sprint. It's an endurance event.

**Hour 1-3:** Fresh. Full energy. The designs are flowing. Every face is a new canvas.

**Hour 4-6:** The rhythm sets in. Hyperfocus engaged. Time starts to dissolve. The ADHD brain is in its element.

**Hour 7-9:** Fatigue starts to creep in. This is where hydration, breaks, and energy management become critical.

**Hour 10-12:** Pure flow state. The body is tired but the mind is locked in. The best work often happens in these late hours.

### Hydration and Breaks

Real talk: you cannot paint for 12 hours straight without proper hydration and breaks.

**My system:**
- Water bottle always within reach
- 10-minute break every 2 hours
- Stretch, walk, breathe
- Snacks: nuts, fruit, energy bars
- No alcohol until the painting is done

### The Moments Between

The magic isn't just in the transformations. It's in the conversations. The connections. The person who sits in your chair and tells you their story. The gratitude when they see themselves glowing under UV.

Those moments fuel the next 12 hours.

### Festival Conditions

Painting at a festival is not the same as painting in a studio. It's dark. It's loud. It's dusty. The ground is uneven. People are dancing, bumping into your workspace, spilling drinks.

You adapt. You protect the kit. You create a bubble of focus in the middle of chaos.

> The ADHD brain doesn't just cope with festival conditions — it thrives in them. Loud music, dark rooms, crowded spaces, constant movement. This is where my neurology performs best.

This is the reality of festival painting. It's beautiful. It's exhausting. It's exactly where I'm meant to be.

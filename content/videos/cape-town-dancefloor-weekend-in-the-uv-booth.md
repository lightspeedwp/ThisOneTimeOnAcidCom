## Home Base Dancefloors

Cape Town is home. Woodstock is my neighbourhood. And on any given weekend, you'll find me in one of three venues with a UV booth, a mirror, and a queue of people waiting to be transformed.

This video follows a typical weekend: Friday at Assembly, Saturday at Modular, afterparty at Truth. Three venues, two nights, and the ritual I've come to love.

### The Venues

**Assembly:** Industrial warehouse in Salt River. Concrete floors, exposed beams, a sound system that shakes your chest. The crowd is young, hungry, and ready to go deep.

**Modular:** Intimate basement club in the city centre. Low ceilings, tight dancefloor, sweat dripping from the walls. This is where the real heads gather.

**Truth:** Afterhours spot in Observatory. Sunrise sessions, people who've been dancing for 12 hours, and the kind of conversations that only happen at 7am.

### The Ritual

Watch the full process: setup at 10pm, first face at 11pm, steady flow until 3am, pack down at 4am.

Every face is different. Every person brings their own energy. Some sit in silence, trusting the process. Others talk the entire time, telling me their life story while I paint sacred geometry across their cheeks.

The music is constant. Driving techno basslines, hypnotic loops, the occasional acid squelch. The ADHD brain locks in and time dissolves.

### The Weekend Rhythm

**Friday:** Fresh energy. The designs are flowing. People are excited, optimistic, ready for the weekend.

**Saturday:** Deeper. The crowd is more committed. These are people who live for the dancefloor, not just visit it.

**Sunday morning:** Transcendent. At Truth, the sunrise light filters through the windows, mixing with the UV glow. People are exhausted but present. The conversations are real.

### Why Cape Town?

This city gave me the scene that shaped my practice. The psytrance festivals raised me, but the underground techno clubs refined me.

Berlin has the history and the infrastructure. Koh Phangan has the beach and the sunshine. But Cape Town has something neither of those places can replicate: **it's home.**

> The dancefloor is my studio, but Cape Town is my foundation. Every face I paint here is a conversation with the community that raised me.

This is a love letter to the Cape Town underground. Thank you for giving me a place to belong.

## Origin Festival 2026

Three days. Over 100 faces painted. A weekend immersed in the psytrance dancefloor, transforming people into walking light sculptures.

### The Festival

Origin Festival is a staple of the Cape Town psytrance calendar. Held in Helderstroom, Western Cape, it's where the South African trance family gathers to celebrate music, community, and creative expression.

### The Art Process

This highlight reel captures the transformation process — from blank canvas to fully realized UV avatar. Watch as geometric patterns emerge, colors layer, and personalities shift under the blacklight.

**Techniques featured:**
- Ambidextrous mirror-image painting
- Neon gradient blending
- Sacred geometry mandalas
- Full-face transformations
- Body art extensions

> The dancefloor is my studio. The bass is my metronome. Each face is a new canvas.

### Music

Set to the driving psytrance basslines that powered the weekend, this video captures the energy, intensity, and pure joy of creating art in festival conditions.

Thank you to everyone who sat in my chair and trusted me with their transformation. You made this weekend unforgettable.

— PSYTRANCE TRIBE —

The original tribe. The foundation. Every other identity Ash has built — artist, cyclist, entrepreneur, grower — traces back to the dancefloor.

The tribe formed at Vortex in 1999 and has grown through twenty-five years of South African outdoor trance, international festivals on four continents, and the Berlin underground. The psytrance tribe is not defined by genre loyalty — it is defined by a shared understanding that the dancefloor is sacred space. This is the tribe that taught Ash to move, to be seen, to stand out on purpose, and eventually to create art that makes others glow.

Key gathering points: Vortex, Alien Safari, Origin Festival, Solipse, AfricaBurn, Boom Festival, The Experience, Universo Paralello, Oregon Eclipse Festival. After 25+ years, his heart now beats for house and techno too — the roots remain, but the branches have grown.

---

— BERLIN UNDERGROUND TRIBE —

Discovered in 2019, just before the world stopped. Berlin attracted misfits, dreamers, and artists from everywhere.

Ash found his species here: DJs, visual artists, fire dancers, sound engineers, fellow festival freaks who understand that creativity isn't a career — it's a way of being. The Berlin tribe overlaps heavily with the psytrance tribe but adds something unique: the techno dimension, the warehouse aesthetic, the flat-cycling-everywhere lifestyle, and a city that doesn't just tolerate weirdness — it demands it.

The tribe's spiritual home is Sisyphos. Ash's favourite venue in Berlin, the one that almost always delivers. Other touchpoints: Hasenheide Park open-airs, Friedrichshain techno nights, Kreuzberg canal-side sessions. The Berlin summer of 2025 — approximately 900km or one million steps over eight weeks — was the tribe's peak expression.

---

— CYCLING TRIBE —

The lifelong physical tribe. Racing since 1994, touring since 2012, daily commuting since always.

This is the tribe that shaped Ash's body and his relationship with distance, discipline, and suffering. Western Province mountain bike champion at sixteen. 7,000+ kilometres touring Thailand. 300-kilometre birthday pilgrimages to Origin Festival. Munich to Amsterdam in ten days. The cycling tribe is defined by the road: the people you meet at rest stops, the shared suffering on mountain passes, the unspoken respect between strangers who choose pedals over petrol.

The Berlin daily circuit is the tribe's meditation: Görlitzer Park to Tempelhof, Spree canal to Tiergarten. The fairy-light-covered bike is the calling card. The loaded touring bike — 40kg, Arkel panniers, Tubus racks — is the identity.

---

— WORDPRESS TRIBE —

The professional tribe. Found at BarCamp Cape Town 2006, cemented through twenty years of WordCamps, open-source contributions, and community building.

The WordPress tribe gave Ash a career, a global network, and a philosophy: open source, community-driven, transparent, accessible. These are the same values his parents instilled. The tribe includes Warwick Booth (since 2006), Barbara Kerr (since 2010), José Abreu (returned 2025), and the three interns selected from 450 applicants. The extended network reaches across four continents through WordCamp conferences, WooConf events, and the daily collaboration of open-source development.

Highlight: speaking at WordCamp Europe 2025 in Basel on "Bridging Design and Development: Figma Design Systems for WordPress Success." The culmination of years building the LSX Design System — the tribe's flagship product.

---

— MUAY THAI TRIBE —

The discipline tribe. Found in Thailand in 2019, with intensive seasons in 2023 and 2025.

The Muay Thai gym on Koh Phangan is a meeting point for digital nomads, fighters, yogis, and people who have opted out of conventional life. The training is brutal and beautiful: pad work, bag work, clinching, sparring. This is the tribe that teaches controlled aggression, body mechanics, and the discipline of showing up when you don't want to.

The Muay Thai tribe connects to the cycling tribe through shared physical suffering and to the dancefloor tribe through rhythm and body awareness. The crossover is natural: the same ADHD brain that craves sensory richness on the dancefloor craves the intensity of the ring.

---

— LIGHTSPEED TRIBE —

The chosen family. Thirteen people who believe in the same values: freedom, quality, continuous learning, and the idea that work should serve life, not the other way around.

Warwick has been here since 2006. Barbara since 2010. José left and came back. The interns are levelling up faster than anyone I've ever seen. This is a tribe that chose to be here — not because they couldn't find work elsewhere, but because the culture, the freedom, and the shared purpose of building excellent things together is worth more than a corporate salary.

The overlap with other tribes is significant. LightSpeed team members attend WordCamps, ride bicycles, go to festivals, and train Muay Thai. The company is not separate from the life — it's integrated into it. The tribe that funds the art. The tribe that makes the freedom possible. The tribe that proves a company can be a family without being a cult.

— CAPE TOWN CREATIVE COMMUNITY —

Cape Town's creative scene is a tribe unto itself. It's where the garden meets the gallery, where the tech community overlaps with the festival scene, where the cyclists and the dancers and the entrepreneurs share coffee shops and co-working spaces and sunset sessions on Signal Hill.

Six Cats Cannabis Club is part of this community — the growers, the plant enthusiasts, the people who approach cannabis as a craft rather than a commodity. The Cape Town creative tribe taught me that entrepreneurship and artistry are not separate paths — they're the same path viewed from different angles.

The mountain defines this city. Table Mountain is the backdrop to everything — the training runs, the sunset sessions, the late-night drives home from parties. The creative community here is shaped by geography: mountains, ocean, vineyards, and bush all within a 30-minute drive. The landscape makes the people expansive. The people make the work meaningful.

Cape Town is where I started. It's where LightSpeed was born, where Six Cats grows, where the cycling tradition began. Every other tribe leads back here eventually. This is the root system.

---

— THE OVERLAP IS THE IDENTITY —

A map of belonging across six continents. The magic happens in the overlap. The WordPress developer who DJs psytrance sets. The trail runner who paints UV faces at festivals. The triathlete who codes during the week and dances all weekend. The Berlin club kid who runs a profitable agency from a laptop. The Muay Thai fighter who grows organic cannabis in a Cape Town garden.

These tribes aren't separate — they're frequencies that, when layered, create something richer than any single identity. The psytrance dancefloor taught me to move. The WordPress community taught me to build. The Berlin scene taught me to be unapologetically myself. The endurance sports taught me discipline. The mountain taught me humility. The bicycle taught me freedom. The Muay Thai gym taught me presence. The LightSpeed team taught me that the right people change everything.

No single tribe defines me. The intersection of all of them does. The overlap is the identity. We are the sum of our tribes. And these are mine.

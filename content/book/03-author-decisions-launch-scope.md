## Purpose and status

This brief defines the intended website for **This One Time on Acid**, based on Ash’s author decisions of 27 October 2026 and the manuscript preparation direction supplied on 3 November 2026. Other dated decisions retain their individually recorded scope and approval state.

It is a planning document, not evidence of implementation, editorial completion or launch readiness. It will inform a later replacement of `content/book/this-one-time-on-acid-brief.md` in `lightspeedwp/ThisOneTimeOnAcidCom`.

Existing issues, the PRD and decision records have not yet been fully reconciled with these refinements. Conflicts must remain visible until their exact amendments are approved. This document does not silently supersede existing access, privacy or release decisions.

## Audience and positioning

The website is explicitly **book-first**.

Its intended audience includes people interested in personal storytelling, creativity, alternative culture and lived experience—particularly misfits, makers, dancers, seekers, festival people and people who feel wired a little differently. Secondary audiences include people familiar with Ash’s creative work and potential collaborators. The final audience description remains subject to refinement.

The primary visitor action is reading the website-specific manuscript after email verification. Newsletter subscription is separate, optional and secondary.

### Book identity

**Working title:** This One Time on Acid

### Recorded access decisions and outstanding delivery evidence

DEC-001 and DEC-003 record free, passwordless online access to the entire website-specific short manuscript after verification by an emailed single-use code, invalidated on use or after 10 minutes.

Returning access lasts a maximum of 30 days per device and ends earlier on sign-out, revocation or clearance of relevant browser storage. Expiry or a new device requires verification again. The browser access credential must contain no email address, and sign-out must invalidate server access.

The purpose-limited reader access record contains only necessary email, verification status, internal reader ID and access-lifecycle timestamps. It is deleted after 12 months without authenticated use. This period does not determine provider delivery, log or backup retention. Passwords, payment and cross-device progress synchronisation are excluded from launch.

DEC-006 defers manuscript offline downloads from Website v1.0 and prohibits intentional chapter caching for offline access. Earlier offline/PWA work remains a later candidate; no issues are cancelled and no later release is committed.

Outstanding delivery work includes provider suitability, transactional email, server-authorised manuscript delivery, device credentials, expiry enforcement, revocation, abuse controls and operational deletion procedures.

Submission alone must not unlock the manuscript. Validation must demonstrate that protected content cannot be obtained through unauthorised requests, reusable public URLs or inappropriate caching. Older submission-based unlocking plans require separately approved reconciliation before implementation.

Ash is the sole authorised internal manuscript-access operator and responsible for deletion requests and verification. Service permissions and a proportionate deletion-request procedure still require verification. Provider processing and support access must be disclosed separately. This allocation does not assign newsletter, contact, telemetry or release responsibilities.

These are accepted policy requirements, not evidence of implementation, successful testing or release readiness. The broader reconciliation wording elsewhere in this brief must not be interpreted as reopening the accepted policy values.

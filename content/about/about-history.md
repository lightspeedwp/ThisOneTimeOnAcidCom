# History

Every milestone in Ash's journey — from a Paarl childhood to international festival stages, filtered by the threads that weave them together.

All milestones in chronological order, colour-coded by category

## The journey so far

### 1981 — Born in Paarl, Western Cape

Ashley Shaw born in Paarl, a small town in the Cape Winelands. The Boland mountains and wide skies of the Western Cape would shape his early sense of space and freedom.

### 1986 — Paarl Junior School

Started at Paarl Junior School. Miss Scott, his primary school teacher, planted the seeds of creativity that would bloom decades later.

### 1993 — First computer: Windows 3.1

Got his first PC at age 12. Self-taught through DOS commands and early software. The start of a lifelong relationship with technology.

### 1994 — Paarl Boys High

Started at Paarl Boys High School. Joined the mountain biking team and the infamous "2 o'clock club" for after-school mischief.

### 1994 — Started racing bicycles

First competitive mountain bike race. The adrenaline was immediate and addictive. This would become a lifelong identity.

### 1995 — First provincial mountain bike race

Competed at provincial level for the first time, testing himself against riders from across the Western Cape.

### 1997 — WP MTB colours — 3rd overall

Earned Western Province mountain bike colours, finishing third overall in the provincial championship.

### 1998 — WP MTB champion — 1st overall

Won the Western Province mountain bike championship outright. Matric year, 17 years old, and already a provincial champion.

### 1999 — Daemelin College — marketing studies

Enrolled at Daemelin College to study marketing while continuing competitive MTB racing at national level.

### December 1999 — First Vortex festival

Attended the December Vortex festival — a life-defining experience. The combination of psytrance, nature, and community rewired everything. This was the moment the dancefloor became a second home.

### Early 2000s — Started yoga practice

Began a consistent yoga practice that would become a cornerstone of his daily routine for the next two decades.

### June 2001 — Solipse solar eclipse festival, Zambia

An 86-hour bus journey from Cape Town to Zambia for a solar eclipse psytrance festival. A pilgrimage that tested everything and rewarded beyond imagination.

### 2003 — Adopted Bart and Lisa

The first two cats joined the household. Bart and Lisa — the beginning of the Six Cats family legacy.

### 2003 — Founded LightSpeed

Started LightSpeed as a one-man IT support company in Cape Town. The seed of what would become a WordPress agency serving clients worldwide.

### 2005 — First Thailand trip — Koh Phangan

Met Mel Heinz in Koh Phangan on the first Thailand adventure. The island would eventually become a seasonal training base years later.

### 2005 — LightSpeed: first employee hired

Hired the first employee at LightSpeed, transitioning from freelancer to team lead.

### 2006 — Started trail running

Began running the mountain trails around Table Mountain and the Boland hills. A new discipline to complement the cycling.

### 2006 — BarCamp Cape Town — WordPress pivot

Attended BarCamp Cape Town and discovered WordPress. Pivoted LightSpeed from IT support to WordPress development. Warwick joined the team that same year.

### 2008 — Media24 — Scrum Master

Joined Media24 as a Scrum Master while running LightSpeed concurrently. Learned agile methodology at enterprise scale.

### 2009 — Chris joined LightSpeed

Chris became part of the growing LightSpeed team, strengthening the development capabilities.

### 2009 — Thailand with Barbara — 40th birthday

Returned to Thailand with Barbara for his 40th birthday. Deepened the connection with Southeast Asian culture.

### 2010 — Started triathlon training

Combined swimming, cycling, and running into triathlon training. Added a new dimension of endurance to an already athletic lifestyle.

### 2010 — Barbara joined LightSpeed as partner

Barbara became a full partner at LightSpeed, bringing business development and client relationship expertise.

### 2011 — Organised WordCamp Cape Town

Co-organised the first WordCamp Cape Town, bringing the WordPress community together in South Africa.

### 2012 — Started bike packing

Transitioned from competitive MTB to loaded touring. The bicycle became a vehicle for adventure, not just racing.

### 2014 — 300km California bicycle trip

Rode 300km through California — Pacific coast roads, redwoods, and sunshine. One of the first major international bikepacking trips.

### 2018 — Started riding to festivals

Combined the two passions: cycling and festivals. Started riding a loaded bike to outdoor dance events instead of driving.

### May 2019 — First Berlin season

Moved to Berlin for the first seasonal visit. Open-air techno, Hasenheide park sessions, and the fairy lights bicycle. The city became a creative anchor.

### May 2019 — Founded Six Cats Cannabis Club

Established the Six Cats Cannabis Club in Woodstock, Cape Town. Named after the six cats. A community garden with cultivation rituals and green philosophy.

### July 2019 — First UV paint experience in Berlin

Picked up UV-reactive face paint at a Berlin psytrance gathering. The neon glowed under blacklight and something clicked. The final creative evolution had begun.

### 2019 — Munich to Amsterdam — 1,000km in 10 days

An epic 1,000km bikepacking journey from Munich through the Alps and Netherlands to Amsterdam in just 10 days.

### September 2019 — Started Muay Thai in Koh Phangan

Began training Muay Thai at a gym in Koh Phangan during the first Thailand training season. Swimming also improved significantly in the island waters.

### September 2019 — Koh Phangan training base established

Koh Phangan became the annual Sep–Nov training base: Muay Thai, swimming, triathlon training, and remote WordPress work from the island.

### 2019 — Hua Hin to Phuket — 900km in 6 days

Rode 900km down the Gulf of Thailand from Hua Hin to Phuket. Monsoon rains, roadside temples, and the open Thai highway.

### February 2020 — First cycling pilgrimage to Origin Festival

Rode a loaded bike to Origin Festival in the Cederberg for the first time. The pilgrimage tradition was born.

### March 2020 — COVID lockdown in Cape Town

The world stopped. Festivals cancelled, borders closed, the dancefloor went silent. But the garden grew, the cats thrived, and the creative fire found new outlets.

### 2020 — Justin rejoined LightSpeed

Justin returned to the LightSpeed team, adding senior development experience during a challenging year.

### August 2020 — First festival painting gig

Painted faces for 12 hours straight at a local gathering in the Western Cape. The energy was electric. The dancefloor had become a canvas.

### 2020 — Lisa passed (age 17)

Lisa, one of the original two cats, passed away at 17. A quiet, steady presence who had been there since the very beginning.

### 2021 — Lourens and Adam joined LightSpeed

The team grew with Lourens and Adam, expanding capacity and diversifying skills.

### 2021 — Started painting ambidextrously

Began training the left hand to paint alongside the right. Double the output on busy festival nights. A signature technique was developing.

### January 2022 — Moe passed; Bean rescued

Moe the cat passed away. Within weeks, Bean arrived as a rescue — life cycling on in the Woodstock garden.

### February 2022 — Origin cycling pilgrimage resumed

Post-COVID, the cycling pilgrimage to Origin Festival resumed. Loaded bike, Cederberg mountains, three days of festival painting.

### May 2022 — Jeff rescued

Jeff joined the Six Cats family — another rescue cat finding his way to Woodstock.

### August 2022 — Ozora Festival — international breakthrough

Painted over 50 unique UV designs at one of the world's largest psychedelic gatherings in Hungary. The international festival painting circuit was open.

### 2023 — Tibi and Zared joined LightSpeed

The LightSpeed team continued to grow with Tibi and Zared joining the roster.

### February 2023 — Birthday sash ride — Origin to Grabouw

Rode from Origin Festival wearing a birthday sash, pedalling through the Cederberg to Grabouw in celebration.

### August 2023 — MoDem Festival — "Atomic Black" style defined

Showcased the "Atomic Black" high-contrast UV design style on main stage dancers at MoDem in Croatia. The signature aesthetic was crystallised.

### October 2023 — Lucy passed

Lucy the cat crossed the rainbow bridge. Named after the Beatles song, she was always the most mysterious of the Six Cats family.

### January 2024 — Book concept born — "This one time..."

Started outlining "This one time..." — a memoir weaving together festivals, faces, bikes, and cats into 20 chapters of South African adventure.

### 2024 — ADHD understanding deepened

A period of deeper self-understanding around ADHD. The hyperfocus that made festival painting possible, the restlessness that demanded movement, the creativity that thrived in chaos — it all made sense.

### 2025 — Nation of Gondwana — UV design evolution

NOG in Brandenburg inspired a new chapter in UV design evolution. The friendliest festival in Germany pushed creative boundaries further.

### 2025 — LightSpeed: Hugo, Brandon, Seren interns

Three new interns joined LightSpeed — Hugo, Brandon, and Seren. Jose also rejoined the team. The agency was thriving.

### 2025 — Miss Scott tribute blog post

Published a tribute to Miss Scott, the Paarl Junior School teacher whose creative encouragement echoed through decades of artistic evolution.

### Summer 2025 — 900km danced in 8 weeks — Berlin record

An extraordinary Berlin summer: 900 kilometres of movement tracked across open-airs, clubs, and Hasenheide park sessions in just 8 weeks.

### June 2025 — WordCamp Europe Basel — speaker

Spoke at WordCamp Europe in Basel, Switzerland. A proud moment for LightSpeed on the global WordPress stage.

### 2025–2026 — AI workflow transformation at LightSpeed

LightSpeed embraced AI-assisted development workflows, integrating tools like Figma Make, Cursor, and Claude into the daily build process.

### February 2026 — Epic 300km Origin birthday ride

40kg pack, 3,200m of climbing, 300km through the Cederberg mountains to Origin Festival. The most ambitious cycling pilgrimage yet — a birthday tradition pushed to the limit.

### February 2026 — Portfolio site launch

Launched the definitive digital home for the Ash Shaw brand — 79 pages, 21 Google Fonts, 33 neon palettes, WCAG AA accessible, and built entirely with Figma Make.

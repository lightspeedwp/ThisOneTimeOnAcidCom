# Berlin

## Berlin — my creative anchor

Every May, Ash arrives in Berlin with a bike and a box of UV paints. The city’s raw underground energy, its open-air summers, and the freedom of cycling everywhere have shaped every aspect of his art. Cape Town is home — but Berlin is where the art finds its sharpest edge.

## Arriving in the capital of freedom

Berlin came to Ash in 2019 — not through brochures or travel blogs, but through stories whispered at afterparties and painted across the faces of returning festival friends. He didn’t arrive with years of planning. He arrived with a bike and the instinct that this city was going to mean something. He was right.

What he found was a city that doesn’t just tolerate difference — it demands it. From Kreuzberg’s graffiti-covered facades to Neukölln’s late-night shawarma runs, Berlin gave him permission to be unapologetically himself. Cape Town would always be home, but Berlin became the city where his art found its sharpest edge.

## The pause and the return (2022)

COVID interrupted everything. Berlin went quiet, the season that had barely started was cancelled, and Ash returned to Cape Town — his permanent home — and sat out the years that swallowed everyone’s plans.

In 2022, he came back. Some venues were gone. Some people had moved on. But the energy was still there, rebuilding itself the way Berlin always does. He picked up exactly where he’d left off: bike, UV paints, open-airs, and the slow warm unfurl of a Berlin summer.

## The underground scene

Berlin’s club culture is legendary, and for good reason. The city’s warehouses, abandoned power stations, and basement bars became Ash’s studios. Under UV lights and surrounded by pounding techno, he discovered that makeup art and electronic music are two expressions of the same energy.

He started painting faces at small open-airs in Hasenheide Park, then at techno nights in Friedrichshain. Word spread through the scene — the guy with the UV paints and the bike covered in fairy lights. Sisyphos became his favourite venue: the one that almost always delivers, where he’s met incredible people and made memories that define his Berlin experience.

## The bike life

Ash doesn’t just cycle in Berlin — he lives on his bike. It’s his primary transport, his meditation, and sometimes his stage. He keeps a dedicated Berlin bike there year-round, separate from his Cape Town bike.

There’s something about the flat, endless Berlin streets that frees the mind. Between Görlitzer Park and Tempelhof, between the Spree canal and Tiergarten, ideas form with every pedal stroke.

## The Berlin family

Berlin attracts misfits, dreamers, and artists from everywhere. Ash found his tribe here — DJs, visual artists, fire dancers, sound engineers, and fellow festival freaks who understand that creativity isn’t a career; it’s a way of being.

The community he’s built isn’t just friends at parties. It’s a support network of neurodivergent creatives who look out for each other, share studio space, and collaborate on wild ideas that would make no sense anywhere else.

## Berlin summers

If you haven’t experienced a Berlin summer, you’re missing one of Europe’s great secrets. From late May, the city transforms into one continuous open-air event. Parks become stages, canals become bars, and every sunset feels like it was art-directed. By September at the latest, it’s time to pack up — the Berlin bike stays, and Ash flies back to Cape Town.

The 2025 summer was a record-breaker: staying with dear friend Vitor, speaking at WordCamp Europe in Basel, practising creative makeup every weekend for two months straight, and dancing approximately 900km over eight weeks. He left with a heart full of joy and love, flying directly to Thailand to cycle down the coast from Hua Hin to Koh Phangan.

## Berlin timeline

_From the first open-air to a seasonal creative anchor_

### May 2019 — First Berlin season

Moved to Berlin for the first seasonal visit. Open-air techno, Hasenheide park sessions, and the fairy lights bicycle. The city became a creative anchor.

### July 2019 — First UV paint experience in Berlin

Picked up UV-reactive face paint at a Berlin psytrance gathering. The neon glowed under blacklight and something clicked. The final creative evolution had begun.

### 2025 — Nation of Gondwana — UV design evolution

NOG in Brandenburg inspired a new chapter in UV design evolution. The friendliest festival in Germany pushed creative boundaries further.

### Summer 2025 — 900km danced in 8 weeks — Berlin record

An extraordinary Berlin summer: 900 kilometres of movement tracked across open-airs, clubs, and Hasenheide park sessions in just 8 weeks.

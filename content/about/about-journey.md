# The journey is the destination

Twenty-seven years of festivals across five continents — from first dancefloor to global nomadic life

From 1999 to now. North America, South America, Europe, Africa, South East Asia. This is the map of a nomadic creative life built on movement, music, and UV paint.

I've never believed in settling. The creative life I've built requires movement — geographic, physical, and mental. This timeline maps the major chapters, cities, festivals, and turning points that shaped who I am and what I create. Not a comprehensive archive, but the moments that mattered.

## The annual rhythm (2019-present)

Since 2019, my life follows a predictable nomadic cycle that fuels creativity through constant change:

### DECEMBER - APRIL

**Cape Town, South Africa**

**Festival season & creative work**

Based in Woodstock, Cape Town. South African summer festival circuit (Origin, Vortex, Forest Trance). UV face painting at events. WordPress development work. Home base with friends, community, and familiar rhythms. The ocean, Table Mountain, and Cape Town's creative energy.

- Festival face painting (Origin, Vortex, local gatherings)
- WordPress client work for LightSpeed
- Ocean swims and Table Mountain hikes
- Woodstock community and creative collaborations

### MAY - JULY

**Berlin, Germany**

**Techno season & European festivals**

Annual pilgrimage to Berlin for European techno season. Multi-day club sessions (Berghain, Sisyphos, Griessmuehle, About Blank). UV face painting in clubs and at European psytrance festivals. Cycling between cities. Reconnecting with Berlin crew and the underground scene that first introduced me to UV art.

- Berlin techno club sessions (Berghain, Sisyphos, etc.)
- European psytrance festivals (Boom, Ozora, smaller gatherings)
- UV face painting in clubs and festivals
- Cycling tours between cities

### AUGUST

**South Africa (cycling route)**

**Bicycle touring & active meditation**

Return to South Africa. Long-distance bicycle touring between festivals or cities. This isn't just transport — it's active meditation, endurance training, and the space where creative ideas crystallize. Solo riding with panniers loaded with UV paints, camping gear, and minimal electronics. The rhythm of pedaling clears mental clutter.

- Long-distance bicycle touring (300-500km routes)
- Solo camping and gear testing
- Mental processing and creative ideation
- Physical endurance building

### SEPTEMBER - NOVEMBER

**Koh Phangan, Thailand**

**Muay Thai training & island life**

Relocate to Koh Phangan for three months. Muay Thai training 5-6 days per week. Triathlon prep (ocean swims, cycling, running). UV face painting at Full Moon, Half Moon, and jungle parties. Remote WordPress work continues. Island community of nomads, fighters, yogis, and creatives. Disciplined routine in a tropical paradise.

- Muay Thai training (5-6 days/week)
- Triathlon training (swim, bike, run)
- UV face painting at Full Moon & jungle parties
- Remote WordPress development work

### NOVEMBER - DECEMBER

**Cape Town, South Africa**

**Festival prep & summer arrival**

Return to Cape Town in time for South African summer. Prep for upcoming festival season. Reconnect with Cape Town crew. Gear maintenance, UV paint restocking, and creative planning for the year ahead. The cycle begins again.

- Festival season preparation
- Gear and paint restocking
- Community reconnection
- Planning upcoming year's travels

## Key milestones and turning points

The moments that changed everything. Not chronological — just significant.

### 2004

#### First psychedelic experience (Lucy)

**Cape Town, South Africa**

**The experience that rewired everything**

The first time I took LSD changed my entire perception of reality, creativity, and what's possible. The geometric patterns I saw in that expanded state became the visual language I'd later paint on faces under blacklight. This wasn't recreational — it was educational. Lucy taught me that consensus reality is one lens among many, and that art can bridge ordinary and non-ordinary consciousness.

### 2019

#### First time holding a UV paintbrush

**Berlin, Germany**

**Found my medium**

A friend handed me UV paints at a small park gathering in Berlin. I'd never painted a face before. No training, no plan, just intuition and blacklight. The person in front of me became a collaborator. We co-created something neither of us expected. That spontaneity, that trust in process — it became my entire method. I've never taken a formal makeup class. Everything I know came from that same approach: experiment, fail, learn, iterate.

### 2020

#### Origin Festival — painted 30+ faces in one night

**Drakensberg Mountains, South Africa**

**Learned stamina and flow state mastery**

Origin Festival taught me endurance. A line of people waiting for UV paint. Hours passing. Hands steady. Focus unbroken. I entered flow state so deep that time dissolved. The music, the night, the faces — all one continuous present moment. That marathon session proved I could sustain creative output for hours without breaking. Stamina became essential to my practice.

### 2021

#### ADHD diagnosis at 40

**Cape Town, South Africa**

**Reframed my entire self-concept**

Getting diagnosed with ADHD at 40 was life-changing. For years I thought I was lazy, undisciplined, or fundamentally flawed. The diagnosis gave me language, understanding, and community. ADHD isn't a deficit — it's a different operating system. Hyperfocus is the superpower behind my intricate UV designs. Pattern recognition is the gift that lets me see geometric possibilities. The restlessness drives constant travel and learning. This 'disorder' is actually my creative engine.

### 2003

#### Started LightSpeed (WordPress agency)

**Cape Town, South Africa**

**Built sustainable creative freedom**

LightSpeed began in 2003 as a small WordPress development operation. Twenty-three years later, it's still running — fully remote, location-independent, sustainably profitable. This agency funds my nomadic creative life. It proved that you can have a 'day job' that supports artistic freedom without crushing your soul. LightSpeed's flexible structure lets me spend months in Thailand or cycle between festivals — as long as the work gets done. This is the foundation beneath everything else.

### 2018

#### Started Muay Thai training

**Koh Phangan, Thailand**

**Embodiment practice changed creativity**

I started Muay Thai training in Koh Phangan in 2018. The discipline, precision, and respect required by the art directly translated to UV face painting. A clean jab is a clean line. A well-timed kick is a perfectly placed gradient. The gym taught me that mastery requires repetition, that technique matters more than force, and that the body is the foundation for all creative work. I can't paint well if I'm disconnected from my body.

### 2017

#### First visit to Berlin

**Berlin, Germany**

**Found creative home and permission to be weird**

Berlin gave me permission to be weird. The city's commitment to nightlife as ritual, its protection of underground spaces, its refusal of mainstream aesthetics — it validated everything I'd been feeling but hadn't articulated. Berlin taught me that clubs can be temples, that dancing for 14 hours is legitimate practice, and that you don't need to explain your weirdness to anyone. This city became an annual anchor.

### 2003

#### Started building WordPress sites

**Cape Town, South Africa**

**Found sustainable income and open source philosophy**

I've been building WordPress sites since 2003, before it was the dominant CMS. The WordPress community taught me open source philosophy: collaboration over competition, sharing knowledge freely, contribution over individual gain. WordCamps became annual pilgrimages. This community showed me that you can give away your best techniques and still thrive. I apply the same principles to UV face painting — share freely, teach openly, trust in abundance.

## The travel map

The cities, countries, and continents that shaped the work. Distance traveled by bicycle, train, plane, and festival shuttle.

### Cape Town

**South Africa**

**Home base**

**1981-present**

Born in Paarl, raised in the Boland, settled in Woodstock, Cape Town. This is home. The ocean, Table Mountain, the South African festival scene (Origin, Vortex, Forest Trance), and the Woodstock creative community. Cape Town is the anchor that makes nomadic life possible — the place I always return to.

- Childhood in Paarl (1981-1999)
- Six Cats cannabis collective in Woodstock (2012-2018)
- South African festival circuit (2019-present)

### Berlin

**Germany**

**Creative pilgrimage**

**2017-present (annual visits)**

Berlin is the city that gave me permission to be weird. First visited in 2017. Returned every May-July since 2019 for European techno season. The underground club culture (Berghain, Sisyphos, Griessmuehle, About Blank, Tresor) taught me that nightlife can be sacred practice. Berlin introduced me to UV face painting and showed me what creative freedom looks like when a city protects its underground.

- First visit and UV paint introduction (2017)
- First Berghain experience (2018)
- Annual May-July techno season (2019-present)

### Koh Phangan

**Thailand**

**Training base**

**2018-present (seasonal)**

Koh Phangan became my September-November training base in 2018. Muay Thai training 5-6 days per week. Triathlon prep. UV face painting at Full Moon, Half Moon, and jungle parties. The island community of nomads, fighters, yogis, and creatives. This is where discipline meets tropical paradise. Three months of structured training creates the physical foundation for the rest of the year.

- First Muay Thai training visit (2018)
- Full Moon Party UV painting sessions (2019-present)
- Annual Sep-Nov training routine established (2020)

### Various

**Portugal**

**Festival circuit**

**2019-present (sporadic)**

Portugal's psytrance festival scene (Boom Festival, smaller gatherings) became part of my European summer rotation. The Portuguese community values transformation, art, and consciousness exploration. Boom Festival (biennial) is one of the world's largest psytrance gatherings — a week-long ceremony in the middle of nowhere. Portugal taught me that festivals can be intentional communities, not just parties.

- First Boom Festival attendance (2022)
- Smaller Portuguese forest gatherings (2019-present)

### Drakensberg Mountains

**South Africa**

**Festival site**

**2019-present (annual)**

Origin Festival happens annually in the Drakensberg Mountains. Multi-day outdoor psytrance gathering in one of South Africa's most dramatic landscapes. This is where I learned festival painting stamina — 30+ faces in a single night, hours of flow state, music and mountain energy converging. The Drakensberg taught me that the land itself is part of the ceremony.

- First Origin Festival (2019)
- 30-face marathon painting session (2020)
- Annual return every December (2019-present)

## The journey continues

This map will keep growing. New festivals, new cities, new chapters. The nomadic creative life isn't about arriving — it's about moving with intention. Every city teaches something. Every festival adds a layer. Every bicycle tour clears mental space for what's next. I'm not searching for a destination. I'm designing a life where the journey itself is the point. Movement is the creative practice. Geography is the curriculum. The UV paint just documents where I've been and who I've met along the way.

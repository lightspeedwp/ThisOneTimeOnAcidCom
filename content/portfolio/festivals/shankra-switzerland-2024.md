# Shankra Switzerland 2024

Aqua cyan and violet purple alpine-inspired design with mountain peak geometric patterns. Return visit to the Swiss festival with evolved technique and bolder color choices that reflect growth and confidence gained through hundreds of faces painted across continents. The Swiss Alps demand designs that echo their majesty—sharp peaks reflecting crystalline clarity, elevated energy matching high altitude intensity. This piece represents the evolution from student to practitioner, from tentative exploration to confident execution, while maintaining deep respect for the mountain environment that hosts us.

# Vortex tribal fusion

Hot pink and violet purple tribal-inspired patterns across the face and shoulders, created during my first major South African psytrance gathering. The design incorporated sacred geometry and fractal elements—each line carrying meaning, each pattern echoing ancient ceremonial markings reimagined for modern consciousness exploration. This piece marked my introduction to the transformative power of the South African psytrance scene, where the energy is different: wilder, more primal, deeply connected to the land itself. Vortex showed me that festival makeup could be more than decoration—it could be ritual.

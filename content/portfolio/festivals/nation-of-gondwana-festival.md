## FAQs

### What UV paints did you use for these rainbow looks?

I used a combination of water-activated neon face paints and alcohol-based body paints for longer wear. The rainbow streaks are layered with a white base underneath to maximise vibrancy under the blacklights.

### How long did these festival looks take to apply?

Most festival looks take 10-20 minutes per person. I keep the designs bold and graphic so they read well from a distance on the dancefloor. Speed is part of the art — the energy and spontaneity of a festival demands it.

# Cyborg renaissance

Futuristic cyborg-inspired design combining organic skin with geometric circuit board patterns in aqua cyan and violet purple. Editorial piece exploring transhumanism and technology themes. The piece investigates the relationship between human identity and technological enhancement in the modern age—asking questions about where flesh ends and machine begins, whether integration diminishes or amplifies our humanity. The circuit patterns follow the natural contours of facial anatomy, suggesting that technology and biology can coexist rather than conflict.

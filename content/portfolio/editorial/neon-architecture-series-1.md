# Neon architecture series 1

Architectural geometric design featuring sharp angular lines in hot pink and royal blue. Studio editorial piece exploring the intersection of makeup art and modernist architecture, with brutalist concrete inspiration. This piece pushed the boundaries of traditional makeup application by treating the face as an architectural canvas—each line representing a structural element, each angle creating negative space that defines form. The brutalist influence comes from Berlin's iconic concrete buildings: angular, uncompromising, and beautiful in their stark honesty.

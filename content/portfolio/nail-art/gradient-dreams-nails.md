# Gradient dreams

Purple to green ombre with metallic finish. Smooth color transition with metallic topcoat showing beautiful color shifts in different lighting conditions—from warm indoor glow to cool outdoor daylight. The gradient technique requires patience and precision: building up layers gradually to achieve seamless transition without harsh lines. The metallic finish adds another dimension, reflecting and refracting light while simultaneously enhancing the underlying color journey from deep violet to vibrant emerald.

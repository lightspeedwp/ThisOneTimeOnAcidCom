# Galaxy nails

Iridescent blues and greens with cosmic shimmer. Cosmic-inspired design with iridescent finish and beautiful shimmer effects that evoke the mystery of deep space and distant nebulae. The technique involves layering translucent colors to create depth—mimicking the way we see through layers of gas and stardust when looking at astronomical photography. Each nail captures a different moment in cosmic time, from the birth of stars to the quiet darkness between galaxies.

## Task
You have just completed a competitive analysis of your product against three competitors. The findings document is complete, with key gaps identified:
- Your product lacks a mobile app (two competitors have one)
- Pricing model is more complex than the market average
- Integration ecosystem is a strength (fewer competitors offer this)

The user says: "Analysis is done. I have solid findings. Now what—should we share these with the team, or should I turn them into action items first?"

As the next-step-options skill, identify the immediate goal and present up to three actionable next steps without executing them.

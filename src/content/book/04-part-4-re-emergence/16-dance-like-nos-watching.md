The psytrance dancefloor is not metaphorically sacred — it's literally sacred. It's where ADHD brains find their natural operating environment. Where sensory richness replaces the boredom of conventional spaces. Where community forms without the filters of daily life. Never about escape — always about deeper engagement with reality.

I don't charge for my makeup. Sometimes people offer money; sometimes I tell them they can buy me a drink. For me it's the pleasure of being able to do my art on somebody and see them happy afterwards. This is a personal art project, not a professional pursuit.

An experience recently: saw someone after not seeing them for two years. I'd done their makeup two years ago. That day, they felt extremely special — everyone kept complimenting them and it made their day. This is the lasting impact: the memory of feeling radiant stays with people long after the paint washes off.

---

I've also explored an alternative book concept called "Dance Like No One's Watching," structured through the visual and identity evolution: early days of dancing, the cow suit, the chicken suit, my rave suit, and finally makeup. Whether it becomes a standalone book or merges with this one remains to be decided.

Every region brings different faces, different skin tones, different relationships to body art. Thai festival-goers embrace full-body UV coverage. Berlin clubbers want sharp geometric precision. South African crowds love bold, expressive colour blocks. I've learned to read and adapt — not changing my style, but letting it be informed by the culture and energy of each place.

The best art happens in the moment. I live for the spontaneous connections on the dancefloor. I look for the radiant souls amplifying the energy. If the vibe matches, we create magic. Festival energy moves fast. Working in these conditions — loud, dark, crowded, constantly moving — is what shaped the ambidextrous technique.

I've started doing nail art too — layered stickers over colour blends, sealed with a glossy topcoat. I call them Fusion Nails. I'm not the best at nail varnish yet, quite messy honestly, but when it comes together the results can be stunning. It's a new creative direction, another way the hands want to make things.

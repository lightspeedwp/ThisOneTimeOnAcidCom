## The Cats

### Timmy *"Timmmaaaahh"*

**Role:** Resident Therapy Cat  

Timmy is the gentle giant of the operation and the sole remaining member of the original six cats. He arrived as a stray, malnourished and cautious, and over months transformed into the most affectionate cat in the house. He has an uncanny sense for when someone needs comfort — he’ll appear on your lap during a stressful work call or curl up next to you when you’re feeling low. He is the unofficial emotional support officer of Six Cats. Every harvest, Timmy claims a spot in the drying room and supervises. His longevity and survival are a testament to resilience.

### Wendy *"Wendells — The Agile Matriarch"*

**Role:** Matriarch  

Wendy was rescued in a rainstorm at just 5 weeks old, tiny and drenched, and brought into the Six Cats family. She grew into a fast, agile, no-nonsense cat who rules the garden with quiet authority, patrolling the perimeter with the precision of a security consultant. The other cats defer to her. She loves playing soccer with rolled-up paper balls — she’ll bat them across the floor for hours if you let her. Wendy’s survival instinct and adaptability mirror the ethos of Six Cats itself — resourceful, resilient, and fiercely independent.

### Jimmy *"Super Slim Jim"*

**Role:** FIV Fighter  

Jimmy tested positive for FIV (Feline Immunodeficiency Virus) when he arrived. The vet said to keep him comfortable. That was years ago. In late 2023, Jimmy suffered a serious illness that nearly took him — but he made a remarkable recovery that stunned everyone. He is still here, still eating, still fighting, still claiming the best sunny spot in the garden every morning. He is proof that a diagnosis is not a destiny. He requires extra care — immune-boosting supplements, regular vet checks, and isolation when he’s run-down — but he gives back more than he takes. Jimmy is the most determined cat we’ve ever known.

### Bean *"The Survivor"*

**Role:** Rescue Cat  

Bean was found in January 2022 at a Wendy house at the local shopping centre, just weeks after Moe passed. His arrival felt like the universe sending a reminder that the cycle of rescue and healing continues. Bean arrived in the worst condition of any cat we’ve taken in — underweight, dehydrated, covered in fleas, and terrified of humans. The first two months were slow — leaving food near his hiding spot, sitting quietly in the same room without approaching, waiting for him to decide when he was ready. When he finally climbed onto Ash’s lap, it was one of the most rewarding moments of the entire Six Cats journey. Bean is now unrecognisable from the cat who arrived. He’s gained weight, his coat gleams, and he greets visitors at the door like he owns the place. Because he does.

### Jeff *"The Wanderer"*

**Role:** Rescue Cat  

Jeff was found in May 2022 and immediately nestled into Ash’s arms with a trust that felt instant and absolute. From that moment, he belonged. Jeff is the adventurer — he disappears for hours, sometimes days, returning with the air of someone who’s been on a tremendous journey and has no intention of telling you about it. He’s the most independent of all the cats, preferring the garden and the neighbourhood rooftops to the comfort of the couch. Jeff taught us that not every personality needs to be domesticated. Some spirits need to roam. You just make sure the door is always open for when they come home.

### Frank *"Blue Eyes"*

**Role:** Stray Visitor  

Frank is striking — pure white with piercing blue eyes that stop visitors in their tracks. He’s the most photogenic of the crew and seems to know it. Frank is gentle, almost regal, and moves through the house with an elegance the other cats lack. He arrived relatively healthy compared to the others, as though the universe decided to send us one cat who didn’t need fixing. Frank is the calm at the centre of the Six Cats operation — beautiful, untroubled, and content.

### Lisa *"Granny Cat"*

**Role:** Original Matriarch  
**In memory:** passed July 2020  

Lisa was one of the original two cats, adopted in 2003 alongside her brother Bart. She lived to a grand old age and earned the nickname Granny Cat in her final years. She was slow, dignified, and had a particular fondness for sitting in the exact spot where you needed to work. Lisa set the tone for everything that followed — the idea that rescued animals deserve not just shelter but a life of comfort and dignity. When she passed, the garden felt emptier for months.

### Moe *"Fat-Boy-Fat"*

**Role:** Couch Commissioner  
**In memory:** passed 4 January 2022  

Moe earned his nickname honestly. He was enormous, unapologetic, and ate with the enthusiasm of someone who had once known hunger and decided it would never happen again. He had one particularly silly trait — he would go absolutely crazy when you blew air at him, spinning and batting at the invisible force like it was the greatest game ever invented. Moe was the comic relief of Six Cats — the cat who got stuck in places, who fell off shelves, who ate food that wasn’t his and looked at you as if to say, "And what are you going to do about it?" Behind the comedy was a cat who had been abandoned and found safety. Moe reminded us that joy is a survival mechanism.

### Lucy *"Dennis the Menace"*

**Role:** Welcoming Committee  
**In memory:** passed 24 October 2023  

Lucy was a cross-Burmese cat who took the role of matriarch after Lisa died. She was incredibly talkative and had a personality that could fill a stadium. She would visit everyone, sit in front of screens demanding attention, tap your face gently with her paw, and reach out to touch customers from a shelf to say hello. She was the welcoming cat — every visitor got the Lucy treatment. Nicknamed Dennis the Menace for her relentless energy, her mischief, and her ability to be in exactly the wrong place at exactly the wrong time. Lucy was the heartbeat of Six Cats. She was curious, demanding, affectionate on her terms, and utterly impossible to ignore. Her passing hit harder than any of the others. The book is dedicated to her. The green garden carries on, but it carries on with Lucy’s spirit woven into every harvest.

## Grades

### Grade AAAA: Quads

*The best of the best!*

This premium cannabis ranks highest in all traits; well cured, with a pungent odour, a sharp flavour, and a smooth, clean-smelling burn & potent smoke. Our hand-selected quads are our best-trimmed buds and cost slightly more than topshelf.

### Grade AAA: Topshelf

*Quality fitting for connoisseurs.*

Topshelf will exhibit an aesthetically pleasing cannabis flower structure, demonstrate desirable terpene aromas and flavours, and have a high cannabinoid content. The bud is dense and in good form. No harsh aftertaste is a good sign of this grade.

### Grade AA: Standard

*Smaller buds than topshelf, but still top quality.*

Standard Grade has many of the same characteristics of Topshelf, but less bud density. Although it is still of excellent quality, many of the same qualities of the Topshelf bud are still present. The THC potency is also still high.

### Grade A+: Preground

*Save time with fine-sieved convenience.*

Stretch your stash with the budtender’s preground cannabis blend. We pass a blend of cannabis through a sieve to remove unwanted plant matter like stalks and seeds, giving you a smooth smoke.

### Grade A: Budget Blend

*Perfect for making edibles.*

Budget grade is great for stretching your bud or making edibles. We do a tight trim on our Standard and Topshelf buds, which yields a lot of powdery goodness. This grade also includes some small popcorn buds — you’re bound to find some gold nuggets in every jar.

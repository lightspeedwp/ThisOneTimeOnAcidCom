# Neon vs Atomic Black

Ash’s podcast exploring the intersection of psytrance art, UV makeup culture, festival life, ADHD creativity, and conversations with the creative community.

## Why a podcast

Ash has always been a storyteller. On the dancefloor, through UV paint, in voice notes sent at 4am to friends across time zones. A podcast is the natural extension of that impulse — a space to share the stories that don’t fit in a photo or a 60-second reel.

The podcast is about depth. It’s about sitting with a topic or a person for an hour and finding the truth underneath the surface. It’s the antidote to the scroll.

## What to expect

Each episode explores one thread of the neon tapestry — a festival memory, a creative technique, a life lesson earned the hard way, or a conversation with someone whose work Ash admires.

Expect honesty. Expect tangents (hello, ADHD). Expect the occasional soundscape from a Zambian bush party or a Berlin dancefloor at dawn. Expect the kind of conversation you’d have around a campfire, not in a studio.

## Who it’s for

Festival souls. Creative misfits. People who’ve ever felt like the world was running on a different operating system. Anyone curious about the intersection of art, neurodivergence, altered states, and the human need to dance.

If you’ve ever painted your face before going out, worn a costume to a party for no reason, or cried on a dancefloor because the music hit different — this podcast is for you.

## Where to listen

The podcast is currently in development, with the first episodes planned for recording in Berlin. It will be available on all major platforms — Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, and wherever you find your audio.

Follow Ash’s social channels for launch announcements. The first season focuses on the people and places that shaped his neon journey — from Cape Town to Berlin, from the first Vortex to the latest Origin.

# Read the first pages

Sample chapters from Ash’s upcoming book. Raw, unfiltered, and straight from the voice notes.

## How to read this

These are draft excerpts. They’re rough, honest, and unpolished by design. The book is being written in the same way Ash paints — fast first strokes to capture the energy, refined later to bring out the detail.

Read them as snapshots of a work in progress. Some passages will make it to the final book, others will evolve beyond recognition. That’s the process.

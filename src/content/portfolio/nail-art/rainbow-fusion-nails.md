# Rainbow fusion

Vibrant multi-color rainbow design using creative sticker art techniques with holographic accents. This piece explores the intersection of traditional nail art and contemporary sticker application—layering different textures, finishes, and colors to create depth and visual complexity. Each nail becomes a miniature canvas, telling its own story while contributing to the larger rainbow narrative across all ten fingers. The holographic accents catch light from unexpected angles, creating prismatic effects that shift with hand movement.

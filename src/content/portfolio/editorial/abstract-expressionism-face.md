# Abstract expressionism face

Avant-garde experimental piece with chaotic splatter patterns in all 8 neon colors. Pure artistic expression without commercial or festival constraints, exploring the boundaries of makeup as abstract art. This session allowed complete creative freedom to treat the face as a canvas for color exploration and spontaneous expression—channeling Jackson Pollock's action painting methodology through cosmetic medium. The chaos is intentional: embracing unpredictability, celebrating accidents, and finding beauty in the uncontrolled collision of pigments.

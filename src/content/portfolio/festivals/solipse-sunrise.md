# Solipse sunrise

Pure yellow and blazing orange radiating patterns emanating from the third eye, designed to mirror the summer solstice sunrise. The piece incorporated sun symbolism and light ray patterns—celebrating the longest day of the year when solar energy peaks and life force reaches maximum potential. Created during a festival that honors transformation through light, this design captures the moment when darkness surrenders to dawn and consciousness expands with the rising sun. Each ray represents a path to awakening, radiating outward from the seat of inner vision.

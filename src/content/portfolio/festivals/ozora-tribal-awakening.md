# Ozora tribal awakening

Hot red and electric green tribal symmetrical design inspired by Hungarian folk patterns and psytrance culture. The piece featured bold geometric shapes and repeating mandala elements that echo traditional Magyar embroidery and decorative arts. Ozora Festival is a pilgrimage site for psytrance culture—the spiritual home where thousands journey each year to reconnect with the source. Every design must honor that sacred energy while paying respect to Hungarian cultural heritage. The symmetry represents balance, the colors represent vitality and growth, the geometry represents universal order.

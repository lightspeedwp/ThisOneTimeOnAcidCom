# Universo Paralello jungle magic

Electric green and blazing orange jungle-inspired patterns with organic leaf and vine motifs. Created on the Brazilian beach during the legendary New Year's psytrance gathering where tropical rainforest meets ocean in an explosion of life force energy. The design mirrors the lush jungle backdrop—vines climbing across skin like they climb through canopy, leaves catching light like they catch morning dew. Universo Paralello demands designs that merge with nature rather than dominate it, becoming part of the ecosystem, surrendering to the wild abundance of Brazilian coastal jungle.

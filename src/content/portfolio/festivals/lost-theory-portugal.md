# Lost theory festival Portugal

Royal blue and electric green flowing patterns inspired by Portuguese ocean waves and coastal psytrance culture. The design featured fluid wave-like movements across the face—mimicking the endless rhythm of Atlantic swells breaking against ancient cliffs. Portugal's coastal festivals have a unique energy where the Atlantic Ocean meets psychedelic trance in perfect harmony. Salt air, crashing waves, and deep bass create an environment where makeup must flow rather than fight—organic, adaptable, constantly in motion like the sea itself.

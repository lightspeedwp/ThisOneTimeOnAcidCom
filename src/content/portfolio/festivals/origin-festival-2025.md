# Origin festival 2026: birthday adventure

It was my birthday weekend, I cycled to the event over two days and then cycled home, I brought all my clothes, hammock gear and makeup bags in panniers on my gravel bike. I danced, celebrated and spread the joy of my makeup. I also had some stickers made.

## FAQs

### Did you really cycle 300km to a festival with a full makeup kit?

Yes! Everything fits into my bike panniers — paints, brushes, stickers, and a hammock. The gravel bike handles the mountain passes and the makeup kit adds about 2kg to the load. Travelling light is part of the adventure.

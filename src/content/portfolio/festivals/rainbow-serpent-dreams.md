# Rainbow serpent dreams

Multi-color serpentine patterns flowing across the face, inspired by the festival's iconic branding and Australian Aboriginal art. Electric green, hot pink, royal blue, and pure yellow combined in flowing organic shapes that echo the Dreamtime stories of creation and connection. This piece honors Indigenous Australian visual culture while celebrating psychedelic festival expression—walking the delicate line between appreciation and appropriation by focusing on universal themes of transformation, nature, and spiritual journey rather than claiming sacred symbols. The serpent represents regeneration, wisdom, and the cyclical nature of existence.

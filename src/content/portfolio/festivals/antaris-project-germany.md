# Antaris Project Germany

Blazing orange and hot red sunset-inspired design created during the peak of summer festival season when European psytrance culture reaches maximum intensity. The piece featured radiating sun patterns with tribal accents—capturing those perfect German summer evenings when the sun hangs low at the horizon for hours, painting the sky in impossible gradients. Antaris Project represents the heart of Germany's outdoor psytrance culture: bold, energetic, unapologetically intense. The design needed to match that energy while honoring the sacred ritual of watching sunset over the main stage.

## The Nomadic Festival Life

Berlin summer 2023. Peak season. Every weekend is a choice between three or four festivals. The bicycle is the vehicle. The city is the playground.

### The Routes

Watch the routes unfold: Görlitzer Park to Tempelhof. Sisyphos to About Blank. The Spree canal path to Hasenheide Park. Through Kreuzberg, Neukölln, Friedrichshain — each neighbourhood a different energy.

The flat, endless Berlin streets free the mind. Between rides, ideas form with every pedal stroke.

### The Loaded Bike

40 kilograms of gear:
- UV paints and brush kit
- Mirror stand
- Camping gear
- Clothes for three days
- Food and water
- Bike tools and spare tubes

Everything fits in Arkel panniers and a bike bag on the top tube. Portability isn't a limitation — it's a design constraint that forces creative efficiency.

### The Festival Circuit

Berlin in summer is festival heaven. Open-airs in abandoned lots. Warehouse parties in industrial zones. Park gatherings under barely-dark skies.

Cycling to a club in fairy lights with a box of UV paints is completely unremarkable in this city. That's why I love it.

### The City as Gallery

Painting faces at open-airs until 3am, cycling home through warm streets that smell of linden trees, and knowing there's another party tomorrow. And the day after. And the day after that.

> Berlin gave me three things no other city could: freedom to be weird, affordable creative space, and the right scene.

This montage captures the rhythm of that summer: movement, art, community, repeat. The nomadic lifestyle powered by two wheels and a city that never asks you to slow down.

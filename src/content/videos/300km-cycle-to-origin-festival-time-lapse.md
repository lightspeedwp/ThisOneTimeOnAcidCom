## The Journey Is the Art

For my birthday in 2026, I cycled 300 kilometres from Woodstock, Cape Town, to Origin Festival in Helderstroom. This time-lapse condenses three days of riding into five minutes of pure visual flow.

### Day 1: Woodstock to Grabouw (80km)

The ride begins in the city. Urban streets give way to mountain passes. Sir Lowry's Pass climbs steeply — legs burning, lungs screaming, but the view from the top makes it worth every pedal stroke.

Arrival in Grabouw: 80 kilometres done. The body is tired but the mind is clear.

### Day 2: Grabouw to Helderstroom (75km)

Birthday ride. The final push to the festival. Through Elgin apple orchards, over Houwhoek Pass, down into the valley where the festival awaits.

Watch the sunset over Elgin — one of the most beautiful moments of the entire journey.

### Day 3: Arrival at the Dancefloor

I arrived at Origin around 11:30 AM on Friday morning, buzzing with endorphins. Skipped the queue, shouted that it was my birthday, and the welcome was overwhelming.

The bike carried everything: camping gear, UV paints, brush kit, mirror stand, clothes, food. 40 kilograms of gear. Everything an artist needs, on two wheels.

### The Return Journey

After the festival, I cycled back to the farm in Elgin to decompress, then all the way back to Cape Town on Tuesday.

Total: just short of 300km with a 40kg bike pack, 3,200m climbing, 14 hours of pure adrenaline, and a top speed of over 75km/h.

> The pilgrimage to a psytrance festival is as important as the event itself. This year, for my birthday, I decided to cycle. It wasn't just a ride; it was a meditation.

Time to dive back into work focus and training for the next adventure. The beat goes on. One love and peace out.

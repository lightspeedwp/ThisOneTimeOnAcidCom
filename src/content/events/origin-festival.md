# Origin Festival

Origin Festival is a premier psytrance gathering nestled between mountains and ocean on Elandskloof Guest Farm in Helderstroom, Western Cape, South Africa. For Ash, it’s more than a festival — it falls on his birthday weekend every year, making the annual pilgrimage a deeply personal tradition. He has cycled to and from the festival four times, carrying a full UV paint kit on a loaded touring bike across mountain passes.

## Location

**Venue:** Elandskloof Guest Farm  
**City:** Helderstroom  
**Country:** South Africa  

Nestled between the Groenland mountains and the coastal fynbos, Elandskloof is a working farm turned festival ground with natural amphitheatres, shaded groves, and star-filled skies.

Let’s go with the Image Gallery & Slideshow first. Since your site is heavy on Portfolio, Videos, and Events, the gallery isn't just a container—it’s the main stage. [1, 2, 3, 4] 
We need styles that move beyond the "basic grid" and into something more dynamic and curated. [5] 
Image Gallery & Slideshow: 10 Funky Style Variations1. The "Bento Chaos" (Asymmetric Grid)

* Visual: A modular grid where images take up different "unit" sizes (1x1, 2x1, 2x2).
* Interaction: On hover, the "units" shift slightly, and the image within scales up to fill its container.
* Best For: Portfolio homepages. [6, 7, 8, 9, 10] 

2. The "Film Strip" (Horizontal Scroll)

* Visual: Images are lined up side-by-side, overflowing the screen width, with visible "perforation" holes on the top and bottom.
* Interaction: Kinetic dragging (mobile-style) or a scroll-wheel-to-horizontal-move transition.
* Best For: Video stills or Event recaps. [11, 12, 13, 14, 15] 

3. The "Scatter Deck" (Interactive Pile)

* Visual: Images appear "tossed" onto the page at random angles and positions, overlapping each other.
* Interaction: Users can click and "drag" images around the screen; clicking one brings it to the front (highest z-index).
* Best For: Creative Portfolio "Moodboards." [16, 17, 18, 19, 20] 

4. The "Infinite Carousel" (Looping Marquee)

* Visual: A seamless, non-stop moving row of images.
* Interaction: The speed increases as the mouse moves to the edges of the screen; hover to pause.
* Best For: Brand logos or Social Media feeds. [21, 22, 23, 24] 

5. The "Accordion Split" (Expansion)

* Visual: Images are thin vertical strips side-by-side.
* Interaction: Hovering over a strip expands it to 60% of the container width while the others shrink.
* Best For: Showcasing 4-5 high-impact Portfolio categories. [25, 26, 27, 28, 29] 

6. The "Magnified Loupe" (Detail View)

* Visual: A clean 2-column grid.
* Interaction: As the mouse moves over an image, a circular "lens" follows the cursor, showing a high-res zoomed-in detail of that specific spot.
* Best For: High-detail Photography or Art. [30, 31] 

7. The "Polaroid Fan" (Nostalgic Stack)

* Visual: A single stack of photos with white borders.
* Interaction: Clicking "Next" slides the top photo off the deck (to the left or right) with a physics-based "flick" animation.
* Best For: Event galleries. [32, 33, 34] 

8. The "3D Carousel" (Depth)

* Visual: Images are arranged in a 3D circle; the "active" image is front and center, while others fade and shrink into the background.
* Interaction: Mouse-wheel rotation with a "blur" effect on the images in motion.
* Best For: Video playlists. [35, 36, 37, 38, 39] 

9. The "Glitch-Transition" Slideshow (Digital)

* Visual: A full-bleed single image.
* Interaction: When the slide changes, the image "fragments" into pixels or RGB-slices before reassembling into the next shot.
* Best For: Music/Podcast visuals or Hero sections. [40, 41] 

10. The "Tiled Reveal" (Pattern)

* Visual: A grid where images are hidden behind solid color tiles.
* Interaction: As the user scrolls, tiles flip 180 degrees (like a game show board) to reveal the images underneath.
* Best For: FAQs or Hidden Features. [42] 

------------------------------
The Gallery Dev Tool: "The Viewport"

* Aspect Ratio Switcher: Test how each gallery handles 16:9 (Video) vs. 9:16 (Social) vs. 1:1 (Insta).
* Gutter Control: A slider to go from "No Gap" (Brutalist) to "Airy" (Minimalist).
* Caption Toggle: Test 5 caption styles: Overlaid (Glass), Underneath (Monospace), or "Tooltip" style. [43, 44, 45, 46, 47] 

Post Type Mapping

* Portfolio: Bento Chaos or Accordion Split.
* Events: Polaroid Fan or Film Strip.
* Videos: 3D Carousel or Glitch-Transition. [48, 49]

Let's get visual and tackle the 10 Image Block Styles. Since your site has Portfolio, Video, and Event post types, the way images are presented shouldn't just be "rectangles on a page"—they should feel like part of the interface's architecture. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 
The Image Block: 10 Funky Style Variations1. The "Organic Blob" (Liquid Mask)

* Visual: The image is masked into a shifting, liquid shape using blob CSS.
* Interaction: The shape "wobbles" or morphs when the mouse hovers over it.
* Best For: Portfolio thumbnails or Podcast guest headshots. [6, 7, 8, 9, 10] 

2. The "Brutalist Frame" (Industrial)

* Visual: A thick, 10px black border with "registration marks" (crosshairs) in the corners and a small "FILE_001.JPG" label in monospace.
* Interaction: On hover, the border changes to a neon color and the image zooms in slightly.
* Best For: Posts and technical Pages. [11, 12, 13, 14, 15] 

3. The "Sticker Peel" (Playful)

* Visual: A white, die-cut border around the image with a subtle drop shadow that makes it look like it's stuck onto the screen.
* Interaction: The corner "peels" back when the mouse approaches, revealing a "Click Me" label underneath.
* Best For: Events and CTA sections. [16, 17, 18, 19] 

4. The "Halftone Screen" (Retro Print)

* Visual: A CSS filter overlay that gives the image a comic-book dot pattern or CMYK offset look.
* Interaction: The halftone dots "wash away" or sharpen into a clear image on hover.
* Best For: Videos or artistic Quotes. [20, 21, 22] 

5. The "Floating Glass" (Depth)

* Visual: The image sits inside a glass-textured frame with a heavy backdrop-filter: blur().
* Interaction: 3D Tilt effect—the image follows the mouse movement in 3D space (tilt.js style).
* Best For: High-end Portfolio pieces. [23, 24, 25, 26, 27] 

6. The "Scanned/Glitch" (Digital)

* Visual: The image is broken into horizontal "slices" that are slightly offset.
* Interaction: A "red-and-blue" 3D ghosting effect triggers every few seconds.
* Best For: Video previews and Podcast art. [28, 29, 30] 

7. The "Polaroid Stack" (Nostalgic)

* Visual: A single image block that looks like three stacked Polaroids.
* Interaction: On hover, the "stack" fans out to reveal three different angles or related images.
* Best For: Portfolio and Events. [31, 32, 33] 

8. The "Neon Glow" (Cyber)

* Visual: The image has no border but a massive, blurred outer glow that matches the image's dominant color.
* Interaction: The glow "pulses" like a neon sign.
* Best For: Videos in Dark Mode. [34, 35, 36, 37, 38] 

9. The "Blueprint Cross-Section" (Technical)

* Visual: Half of the image is a regular photo; the other half is a blue-and-white "schematic" or wireframe sketch.
* Interaction: A vertical slider allows the user to reveal more of the "blueprint" side.
* Best For: Portfolio case studies. [39] 

10. The "Window Blind" (Transition)

* Visual: The image is hidden behind vertical or horizontal slats.
* Interaction: The slats "open" (rotate 90 degrees) when the user scrolls to the block.
* Best For: Dramatic reveals on Pages. [40, 41] 

------------------------------
Dev Tool: The "Image Lab" Sub-Page

   1. Aspect Ratio Toggle: Test each of the 10 styles in Square (1:1), Landscape (16:9), and Portrait (4:5).
   2. Filter Gallery: A "Quick-Apply" sidebar for CSS filters (Grayscale, Sepia, Invert, Hue-Rotate) to test with the styles.
   3. Caption Styles: 5 ways to show text on the image (e.g., floating pills, bottom-aligned ribbons, or "hover-only" overlays). [42, 43, 44, 45, 46] 

Post Type Mapping

* Portfolio: Use Style 01 (Blob) and Style 05 (Glass) for a curated, premium feel.
* Videos: Use Style 08 (Neon) and Style 06 (Glitch) to lean into the digital medium.
* FAQ/Posts: Use Style 02 (Brutalist) to keep things structured but stylish.
